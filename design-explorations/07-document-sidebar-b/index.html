About

A short introduction

I build software and prototypes, usually around ideas that begin as a useful irritation: a missing tool, a confusing interface, a question with no good place to put it. The work tends to be practical, but I care a lot about how it feels to encounter.

This site is a home for selected projects, notes from the process, and the occasional thought that benefits from being finished enough to share.
